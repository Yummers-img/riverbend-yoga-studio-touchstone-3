Gentle Yoga

A slower-paced class focused on comfortable stretching, balance, breathing, and mobility. Beginners and older adults are welcome.

Vinyasa Yoga

A flowing class that connects movement with breathing. Students can choose modifications based on their experience and comfort level.

Restorative Yoga

A quiet practice using supported poses and relaxation techniques to help reduce tension and encourage rest.

Private Sessions

Private sessions provide individual guidance for students who want personalized instruction, help learning poses, or a practice designed around their goals.

Pricing Overview
